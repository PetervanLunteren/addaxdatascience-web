Common questions

Does my data get uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything runs on your own computer, with no internet needed after install. Your photos never leave your machine.

Will our data be used to train models?

No. AddaxAI runs entirely on your own computer. Your images are never uploaded, shared, or used to train any model.

Can I use it for commercial work?

It depends on the model. AddaxAI itself is MIT-licensed, so free for commercial use, but each species model has its own licence, and some are non-commercial. Check the licence of any model you plan to use, in the model zoo or under Model info in the app.

How accurate is it?

It depends on the model and how closely it matches your species, cameras and setup, so test it on your own data first. And because you check and correct the labels yourself, your final dataset is as accurate as you make it.

Do I need a GPU?

No. It runs faster with an NVIDIA GPU or Apple Silicon, but it works on any computer, just slower.

Does it work on video?

Yes. It handles both photos and videos.

Which systems does it run on?

Windows, macOS and Linux. On Mac it needs Apple Silicon, a model with an M1, M2, M3 or M4 chip (roughly 2020 or newer); older Intel Macs are not supported. Not sure which you have? Open the Apple menu and click About This Mac: if it lists a Chip, you are set; if it says Intel, it will not run.

How do I cite AddaxAI?

The citation is on GitHub. Please also cite every model you used; each model's citation is inside AddaxAI, under Model info.
